POD Automation

From 5 Hours to 15 Minutes: Automating POD Collection

Eliminating the biggest operational bottleneck in freight brokerage

The Problem

POD collection was the biggest bottleneck. Two full-time staff spent their entire day logging into 6 different carrier portals, downloading PODs, and uploading them to the TMS. Delays averaged 4.8 days, causing invoice holds.

The Automation

RPA bots were configured for all 6 shipper portals (Walmart, Amazon, Target, Home Depot, Kroger, Wayfair). Bots run on schedule, pull PODs automatically, validate them with AI, and route to billing.
