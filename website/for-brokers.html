Frequently Asked Questions

FreightIQ can be configured and deployed in 48–72 hours. Our team handles the full setup including RPA bot configuration, carrier portal integrations, and AI model training on your historical data.

FreightIQ integrates with major TMS platforms and offers API access on Professional and Enterprise plans. We also support CSV import/export for systems without direct API integration.

Bots use encrypted credential storage and secure automation to log into carrier portals. All credentials are stored in enterprise-grade vaults and never exposed in logs or UI.

FreightIQ has automatic retry logic, fallback workflows, and instant alerts for bot failures. Our dashboard shows real-time bot health and every run is logged for audit purposes.

We support 6 major shipper portals out of the box (Walmart, Amazon, Target, Home Depot, Kroger, Wayfair) and can build custom bots for additional portals on Enterprise plans.

FreightIQ uses enterprise-grade encryption, SOC 2 compliant infrastructure, and role-based access control. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
