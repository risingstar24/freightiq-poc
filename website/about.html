Our Mission

Why We Built FreightIQ

Freight brokerage is one of the last industries where manual work still dominates. Phone calls to track loads. Spreadsheets to manage carriers. Faxes to receive documents. Hours spent logging into a dozen portals just to collect proof of delivery.

We built FreightIQ to change that. AI and RPA can handle 80% of the repetitive tasks that consume your team's time every day — freeing brokers to focus on what actually grows the business: relationships, strategic accounts, and service quality.

Our platform isn't about replacing freight brokers. It's about multiplying what a small, focused team can achieve. With FreightIQ, 3 people can do what previously required 8 — and do it with better data, better decisions, and better outcomes.
